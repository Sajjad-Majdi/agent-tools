# <Video name>: scene brief for helper agents

A narrated Persian explainer (or promo): <topic>, about <N> minutes. Remotion 4 project, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Narration
audio and per-sentence timing already exist; you only write the VISUALS of your scenes, synced to the narration beats.

## The viewer
<Who watches and what they already know. For a zero-to-hero explainer: they know nothing.> Every scene must make the idea
visibly obvious: concrete numbers, objects that move and transform, one focal point at a time, cause then effect. Show,
do not list. Avoid text walls; short labels only.

## Hard rules
1. **Technical terms on screen are English, in Latin script** (`Token`, `Embedding`, `Attention`, `KV Cache` ...). Never
   transliterate them into Persian letters. Persian UI text is fine for plain words.
2. Persian labels: short, colloquial, correct. Use `FaText` (RTL, Vazirmatn). Mixed Persian and English inside one `FaText` is
   fine.
3. Layout: the title bar occupies y < 190 (drawn by `SceneShell`). Put all your content in x 90..1830, y 200..1000. No
   captions or subtitles are drawn. Minimum text size about 22 px (at 1920 wide); main labels 28-40 px.
4. Every visual element appears or transforms in sync with the sentence that talks about it: use
   `const at = useAt(); at(k, delayFrames?, durFrames?)` for an eased 0..1 progress starting at beat `k` (sentence k starts).
   `useSince()(k)` gives frames since beat k. Fade old material out when the narration moves on (`1 - at(k)`), do not pile
   up.
5. Animation only through `useCurrentFrame`, `interpolate` and the helpers. No CSS transitions or CSS animations. Be
   deterministic: use `rand(i)` from the kit, never `Math.random`.
6. Dark theme, palette `C` from the kit. Fonts: `FA` (Persian), `EN` (Inter), `MONO`.
7. Do NOT edit `kit.tsx`, `rootHelper.tsx`, the root composition file, the narration, timing or glossary files, or any
   scenes file other than yours. Put any helper components you need inside your own scenes file.
8. Do not render the full video. Do not start a preview server or open a browser preview.

## Kit (`src/<name>/kit.tsx`): read it first
`SceneShell` (background, title, progress, audio), `useAt`, `useSince`, `mix`, `rand`, `Reveal`, `Block` (labelled box),
`Arrow` (animated, optional curve or dashes), `Chip`, `FaText`, `Cross`, `Tokens` (row of word chips), `Formula` (mono
text), `Matrix` (cell grid with value(r,c) 0..1, or below 0 for hidden). Look at the existing scenes files for style and
patterns.

Scene component signature: `export const Foo: React.FC<SP> = (p) => (<SceneShell {...p} title="Persian title, English terms
in Latin" en="ENGLISH SUBTITLE"><FooBody/></SceneShell>)`, where `FooBody` uses the hooks. The `SP` type is exported from
the first scenes file.

## Tools
- Sentences and the start frame of each beat: `node scripts/beats.mjs <sceneId>` (full text also in
  `src/<name>/timing.json`).
- Render stills of YOUR scenes only, through your own entry file (isolated bundle):
  `node scripts/stills.mjs <outDir> src/entries/<X>.tsx T-<sceneId>:<frame> ...`. Then LOOK at the PNGs. Check several
  frames per scene (early, middle, near the end).
- Type check: `npx tsc --noEmit` (fix only the errors in your own file; others may be mid-edit by other agents).

## Quality bar before you report done
For each scene: every sentence has a matching visual moment; nothing overlaps or clips off-screen; no leftover elements
clutter later beats; English terms are in Latin; text is readable; stills are checked with your own eyes. Report: scenes
done, anything you are unsure about, and one or two still paths per scene.

Subagent budget: at most 3 agents running at the same time in total (including nested ones), effort high or lower.
